Motivation & Objectives

Colombia's fintech sector is growing rapidly, yet many institutions lack accessible tools to analyze customer behavior and predict churn. This project aims to:

  • Democratize analytics — enable non-technical stakeholders to explore customer data through interactive visualizations
  • Identify behavioral segments — uncover distinct customer groups via unsupervised clustering on transactional features
  • Predict churn risk — build interpretable models that quantify churn probability and surface key drivers
  • Enable what-if analysis — let decision-makers simulate interventions and assess impact on retention
CORE QUESTION

"How can interactive visual analytics help Colombian fintech institutions understand, segment, and retain their customers?"

Methodology & Approach

We follow a 5-stage analytical framework that progresses from exploration to actionable simulation:

1EXPLORE— EDA with interactive charts & maps
2CONFIRM— Hypothesis testing via ggstatsplot
3SEGMENT— K-Means, Hierarchical, PAM clustering
4CALIBRATE— Lasso logistic regression for churn
5SIMULATE— What-if analysis with real-time sliders

Key Findings

Seasonal patterns differ by transaction type — Transfers peak mid-year while deposits show end-of-year spikes, confirmed by Kruskal-Wallis tests (p < 0.001).

Income brackets drive behavioral divergence — High-income customers average 3.2x more transaction volume than low-income segments, with significantly different product usage.

Geographic clustering of churn risk — Rural departments show 40% higher churn probability than urban centers like Bogotá and Medellín, linked to limited product access.

Satisfaction score is the #1 churn predictor — Lasso feature selection consistently ranks satisfaction_score as the strongest predictor, followed by tenure and active_products.

Product bundling reduces churn ~15% — What-if simulations show increasing active_products from 1→3 cuts predicted churn from ~47% to ~32%.

Discovered Customer Segments

Optimal clustering (k=4) reveals four distinct behavioral profiles across 10 engineered features:

Power Users~18% of customers

High frequency, high value, long tenure. Low churn risk. Cross-sell opportunities.

Regular Users~35% of customers

Consistent activity, moderate values. Stable segment. Upsell potential via engagement.

Occasional Users~28% of customers

Sporadic engagement, low product count. Respond well to targeted campaigns.

At-Risk / New Adopters~19% of customers

Very low activity, short tenure, high churn probability. Priority for onboarding programs.

Conclusion & Recommendations

COFINFAD Analytics demonstrates that interactive visual analytics can transform raw fintech data into actionable business intelligence.

1

Retain power users — Deploy loyalty programs with real-time satisfaction monitoring dashboards

2

Intervene on at-risk segments — Personalized outreach and product bundling within 90-day onboarding

3

Expand underserved regions — Region-specific campaigns for rural Colombian departments with high churn

4

Leverage what-if simulations — Use the simulator to quantify ROI of retention initiatives before deployment

Future Work

  • Integrate additional data sources (e.g., RFM scoring, external economic indicators)
  • Develop methods to proactively identify network effects among customer clusters
  • Expand prediction module with ensemble models (Random Forest, XGBoost)
  • Add real-time data streaming for live dashboard monitoring
  • Incorporate NLP analysis on support ticket text for deeper churn insights
