query_stats SECTION 01

EDA Insights探索性分析洞察

Geographic Patterns Across Departments各省份的地理模式

Spatial analysis of customer distribution and transaction behavior across Colombia’s 15 departments reveals significant geographic heterogeneity. Urbanized departments such as Bogotá and Antioquia concentrate the majority of fintech users and aggregate transaction volume, while rural and peripheral regions exhibit distinct behavioral profiles characterized by lower engagement and higher churn propensity. These geographic patterns reflect the broader urban-rural digital divide and carry direct implications for region-specific product design and service delivery strategies.

对哥伦比亚15个省份的客户分布和交易行为进行空间分析，揭示了显著的地理异质性。波哥大和安蒂奥基亚等城市化省份集中了大多数金融科技用户和总交易量，而农村和边缘地区则表现出以较低参与度和较高流失倾向为特征的独特行为特征。这些地理模式反映了更广泛的城乡数字鸿沟，对区域特定的产品设计和服务交付策略具有直接启示。

Income Bracket Differences in Transaction Behavior不同收入层级的交易行为差异

Disaggregating transaction metrics by income bracket uncovers meaningful differences in how customers across the economic spectrum interact with fintech products. Higher-income customers tend toward greater transaction frequency, higher average values, and broader product adoption, while lower-income segments show more concentrated usage patterns with fewer active products. However, the relationship between income and behavior is not strictly linear—middle-income brackets often display the highest variability, suggesting that factors beyond income alone shape transactional engagement.

按收入等级分解交易指标，揭示了不同经济水平的客户与金融科技产品交互方式的显著差异。高收入客户倾向于更高的交易频率、更高的平均交易金额和更广泛的产品采用，而低收入群体则表现出更集中的使用模式，活跃产品较少。然而，收入与行为之间的关系并非严格线性——中等收入群体通常表现出最高的变异性，表明除收入之外的其他因素也在影响交易参与度。

Temporal Trends in Monthly Transaction Volumes月度交易量的时间趋势

Temporal decomposition of the 12-month observation window reveals pronounced seasonality in transaction volumes. Transfer volumes spike at year-end—driven by holiday remittances, end-of-year bonuses, and fiscal settlement cycles characteristic of Colombian payroll calendars. Payment volumes follow a distinct mid-year peak coinciding with the “prima de servicios” period and back-to-school spending. Deposit volumes remain comparatively stable, suggesting habitual savings behavior. These patterns have direct implications for infrastructure capacity planning, fraud detection thresholds, and customer support staffing.

对12个月观察期的时间分解揭示了交易量中显著的季节性特征。转账量在年末激增——受假日汇款、年终奖金和哥伦比亚薪资日历特有的财政结算周期驱动。支付量在年中达到高峰，与"服务奖金"期和开学消费时间吻合。存款量相对稳定，表明存在习惯性储蓄行为。这些模式对基础设施容量规划、欺诈检测阈值和客户支持人员配置具有直接影响。

Correlation Patterns Between Demographic and Behavioral Variables人口统计与行为变量之间的相关性模式

Correlation analysis between demographic attributes (age, income bracket, department, tenure) and behavioral features (transaction volumes, product usage, engagement scores) identifies both expected and non-obvious associations. Tenure and active product count show a moderate positive correlation, while satisfaction scores correlate more strongly with engagement frequency than with raw transaction volume. These inter-variable relationships inform feature selection for downstream clustering and prediction models and highlight which demographic proxies carry genuine behavioral signal.

人口统计属性（年龄、收入等级、省份、客户期限）与行为特征（交易量、产品使用、参与度评分）之间的相关性分析识别出了预期的和非显而易见的关联。客户期限与活跃产品数量呈中度正相关，而满意度评分与参与频率的相关性强于与原始交易量的相关性。这些变量间关系为下游聚类和预测模型的特征选择提供了依据，并突出了哪些人口统计代理变量承载了真正的行为信号。

Statistical Test Results统计检验结果

One-way ANOVA confirms that average transaction values differ significantly across income brackets (p < 0.001), though the corresponding effect size (η²) is small—a direct consequence of the large sample size (n = 48,723) where even trivially small differences achieve statistical significance. Independent samples T-tests further reveal significant differences in satisfaction scores between churned and retained customers. These results underscore a critical methodological point: statistical significance does not equate to practical significance, and business decisions must weigh effect sizes alongside p-values.

单因素方差分析确认不同收入等级的平均交易金额存在显著差异（p < 0.001），但相应的效应量（η²）较小——这是大样本量（n = 48,723）的直接结果，在大样本下即使微不足道的差异也能达到统计显著性。独立样本T检验进一步揭示流失客户与留存客户之间满意度评分的显著差异。这些结果强调了一个关键的方法论要点：统计显著性不等于实际显著性，商业决策必须同时权衡效应量和p值。

hub SECTION 02

Clustering Insights聚类分析洞察

Optimal k Selection最优k值选择

Determining the optimal number of clusters employs three complementary validation methods. The elbow plot of within-cluster sum of squares (WSS) identifies the inflection point where additional clusters yield diminishing returns in inertia reduction. Silhouette analysis measures how well each observation fits its assigned cluster versus the nearest neighboring cluster, with the highest average silhouette width indicating the best-separated solution. NbClust aggregates votes across 30 indices to provide a consensus recommendation. The convergence of all three methods on the same k value provides strong evidence that the selected cluster count balances interpretability with fidelity to the underlying data structure.

确定最优聚类数量采用三种互补的验证方法。组内平方和（WSS）的肘部图识别出额外聚类带来递减收益的拐点。轮廓分析衡量每个观察值与其所属聚类的匹配程度与最近邻聚类的比较，最高平均轮廓宽度表示最佳分离方案。NbClust汇总30个指标的投票以提供共识推荐。三种方法在同一k值上的收敛为所选聚类数量在可解释性与对底层数据结构的忠实度之间取得平衡提供了有力证据。

Algorithm Comparison: K-Means, Hierarchical, PAM算法比较：K-Means、层次聚类、PAM

Applying multiple clustering algorithms to the same standardized feature space allows for a robust comparison of segmentation stability. K-Means optimizes for spherical, equal-variance clusters and performs well when segments are roughly balanced in size. Hierarchical clustering (Ward’s method) builds a dendrogram that reveals nested structure and is less sensitive to initialization. PAM (Partitioning Around Medoids) uses actual data points as cluster centers, making it more robust to outliers than K-Means. Comparing the resulting partitions across algorithms—via metrics such as adjusted Rand index and cluster membership overlap—reveals which segments are algorithmically stable and which are artifacts of a specific method’s assumptions.

将多种聚类算法应用于相同的标准化特征空间，可以对分割稳定性进行稳健比较。K-Means针对球形、等方差聚类进行优化，当各群体规模大致均衡时表现良好。层次聚类（Ward法）构建树状图，揭示嵌套结构，对初始化不太敏感。PAM（围绕中心点划分）使用实际数据点作为聚类中心，比K-Means对离群值更具鲁棒性。通过调整兰德指数和聚类成员重叠度等指标比较各算法的分区结果，可以揭示哪些分割是算法稳定的，哪些是特定方法假设的产物。

聚类揭示了COFINFAD客户群体中不同的客户原型，每种原型以交易行为、产品参与度和态度指标的独特组合为特征：

  • bolt 高活跃用户 — 高交易频率、广泛的产品采用、较高的满意度和最低的流失概率。这些客户构成了收入的支柱。
  • warning 高风险用户 — 曾经的高价值客户，表现出满意度下降和近期参与度降低。该群体是最紧迫的干预目标。
  • bedtime 沉睡用户 — 所有指标的活动度较低，长时间不活跃，产品使用极少。许多用户可能已经实质性流失。

Key Differentiating Features Between Segments细分群体之间的关键区分特征

Radar chart and heatmap profiling of cluster centroids identifies the features that most sharply distinguish one segment from another. Active product count, satisfaction score, and engagement frequency emerge as the primary discriminators, while raw transaction volume and demographic attributes contribute less to inter-cluster separation. This feature importance ranking from clustering aligns with and reinforces the variable selection performed independently by the Lasso prediction model, providing cross-module validation of which customer attributes carry the most analytical signal.

雷达图和聚类中心热力图分析识别出最能区分不同群体的特征。活跃产品数量、满意度评分和参与频率是主要的区分因素，而原始交易量和人口统计属性对聚类间分离的贡献较小。聚类得出的特征重要性排名与Lasso预测模型独立执行的变量选择一致且相互加强，提供了对哪些客户属性承载最多分析信号的跨模块验证。

Business Implications of Each Segment每个细分群体的商业影响

Each segment demands a differentiated business strategy. Power Users warrant loyalty programs and proactive satisfaction monitoring to prevent value erosion. At-Risk customers require targeted re-engagement campaigns combining personalized outreach with product bundling incentives. Dormant users may respond to reactivation offers emphasizing product discovery and simplified onboarding. New Adopters need guided lifecycle management—structured onboarding sequences, early cross-sell prompts, and satisfaction check-ins—to accelerate their progression toward multi-product adoption and long-term retention.

每个细分群体需要差异化的商业策略。高活跃用户需要忠诚度计划和主动的满意度监控以防止价值流失。高风险客户需要结合个性化推广和产品捆绑激励的定向再参与营销活动。沉睡用户可能对强调产品发现和简化引导的激活优惠有所回应。新采用者需要引导式生命周期管理——结构化的引导流程、早期交叉销售提示和满意度回访——以加速其向多产品采用和长期留存的转变。

Prediction Insights预测模型洞察

Top Churn Predictors Identified by LassoLasso识别的主要流失预测因子

Lasso (L1-regularized) logistic regression performs automatic variable selection by shrinking uninformative coefficients to exactly zero. The surviving predictors with non-zero coefficients identify the key drivers of customer attrition:

  • sentiment_satisfied satisfaction_score — The single most influential predictor. Low satisfaction is the strongest signal of impending churn.
  • thumb_up nps_score — Net Promoter Score acts as a complementary attitudinal signal. Detractors exhibit markedly higher churn rates.
  • inventory_2 active_products — Product breadth creates switching costs. Customers using three or more products are significantly less likely to leave.
  • support_agent support_tickets_count — High support ticket volumes signal unresolved friction and are associated with incremental increases in churn probability.

Notably, raw transactional volume features (total transfers, deposits, payments) are largely zeroed out by the Lasso penalty, indicating that how much a customer transacts is less predictive of churn than how they feel about the service and how broadly they use the product suite.

Lasso（L1正则化）逻辑回归通过将无信息系数压缩至零来执行自动变量选择。具有非零系数的存活预测变量识别出客户流失的关键驱动因素：

  • sentiment_satisfied satisfaction_score — 最具影响力的单一预测因子。低满意度是即将流失的最强信号。
  • thumb_up nps_score — 净推荐值作为补充性态度信号。贬损者表现出明显更高的流失率。
  • inventory_2 active_products — 产品广度创造了转换成本。使用三种或更多产品的客户显著不太可能离开。
  • support_agent support_tickets_count — 高工单量表明未解决的摩擦，与流失概率的递增相关。

值得注意的是，原始交易量特征（总转账、存款、支付）在Lasso惩罚下基本被压缩为零，表明客户交易量的多少不如他们对服务的感受和产品使用广度更能预测流失。

Lasso vs Ridge Model ComparisonLasso与Ridge模型比较

Comparing Lasso (L1) and Ridge (L2) regularized logistic regression reveals complementary strengths. Lasso produces a sparse model by eliminating irrelevant predictors entirely, yielding superior interpretability and identifying the minimal set of features that drive churn. Ridge retains all predictors with shrunken but non-zero coefficients, often achieving marginally better predictive accuracy when features are correlated. In the COFINFAD context, both models converge on the same top predictors (satisfaction, NPS, active products, support tickets), but Lasso’s explicit variable selection makes it the preferred choice for business communication and actionable insight generation.

比较Lasso（L1）和Ridge（L2）正则化逻辑回归揭示了互补优势。Lasso通过完全消除不相关的预测变量产生稀疏模型，具有卓越的可解释性，并识别出驱动流失的最小特征集。Ridge保留所有预测变量但系数缩减为非零值，在特征相关时通常能实现略好的预测准确性。在COFINFAD背景下，两种模型在相同的顶级预测变量（满意度、NPS、活跃产品、工单）上趋于一致，但Lasso显式的变量选择使其成为商业沟通和可操作洞察生成的首选。

Key Drivers of Churn Probability流失概率的关键驱动因素

Coefficient analysis from the regularized models reveals a clear hierarchy of churn drivers. Satisfaction score and NPS operate as attitudinal indicators—they capture how the customer feels about the service. Active product count and engagement frequency represent behavioral indicators—they reflect how deeply the customer is embedded in the platform ecosystem. Support ticket count serves as a friction indicator—it signals unresolved pain points. The interplay of these three dimensions (attitude, behavior, friction) provides a comprehensive framework for understanding and intervening on churn risk at the individual customer level.

正则化模型的系数分析揭示了流失驱动因素的清晰层次。满意度评分和NPS作为态度指标——捕捉客户对服务的感受。活跃产品数量和参与频率代表行为指标——反映客户在平台生态系统中的嵌入深度。工单数量作为摩擦指标——信号未解决的痛点。这三个维度（态度、行为、摩擦）的相互作用提供了在个体客户层面理解和干预流失风险的综合框架。

探索性分析中识别的流失风险地理集中度与高风险和沉睡聚类的空间分布精确吻合。平均流失概率较高的农村省份恰好是这些高风险群体过度集中的地区。人口统计交叉列联进一步揭示，收入等级与聚类成员的关联反映了预测模型检测到的收入-流失相关性，提供了对同一底层现象的多方法三角验证。

Integrated Customer Understanding整合的客户理解

Prediction quantifies individual-level churn risk and identifies the specific levers available for intervention. Together, these three layers enable a progression from understanding (EDA) to segmentation (clustering) to action (prediction), forming a complete analytical pipeline for customer lifecycle management.

将EDA洞察、聚类画像和预测模型输出相结合，产生了每位客户的360度全景视图。EDA提供宏观背景——季节性模式、地理分布和人群层面趋势。聚类将每位客户分配到具有相关商业含义的行为原型。预测量化个体层面的流失风险并识别可用于干预的具体杠杆。这三个层次共同实现了从理解（EDA）到分割（聚类）再到行动（预测）的递进，形成了客户生命周期管理的完整分析管道。
